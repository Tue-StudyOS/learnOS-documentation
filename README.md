# learnOS - User Research

Supporting research materials for our *Project Journey Portfolio - learnOS*, developed in the StudyOS course. We explored how students prepare for exams and how teaching staff identify and respond to learning difficulties.

## Main insight

Students needed clearer priorities and earlier recognition of knowledge gaps. Teaching staff needed better visibility into difficulties that correct-looking, AI-assisted submissions could conceal. These findings informed our study-support skills and chat-analysis component.
